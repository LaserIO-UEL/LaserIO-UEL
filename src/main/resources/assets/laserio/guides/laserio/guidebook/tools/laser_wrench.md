# Laser Wrench

The Laser Wrench is primarily used to connect multiple Blocks together.

[Nodes](../blocks/laser_node.md) and [Connectors](../blocks/laser_connector.md) can be connected by holding shift and right-clicking to select a block, and then right-clicking another block to form a connection.

The max connection range is 8 blocks.

## Laser Wrench

*The currently selected block is highlighted green.*

![](laserio:assets/tools/laser_wrench/laser_wrench.png)

Connecting Nodes one by one after you placed them can be tedious; luckily the Laser Wrench can automatically do that for you!

While placing Nodes, holding the Laser Wrench in your offhand automatically connects each Node to the previously placed one!

Note: This feature works with (Advanced) Laser Connectors as well!
