# Card Cloner

The Card Cloner can be used to 'copy and paste' card settings from one card to another! **This functionality has also been extended to [Laser Nodes](../blocks/laser_node.md)!**

When pasting, [Overclockers](../upgrades/overclocker_card.md) and [Filters](../filters/filters.md) will be automatically taken (or placed back) inside the currently available [Card Holder](card_holder.md). These items will not be pulled out of the player's inventory.

If any of the necessary items are missing, the paste will fail. It will NOT partially paste the settings.

## Card Cloning

From within a [Laser Node](../blocks/laser_node.md) screen, pickup the Card Cloner (on your cursor) and left-click on a card to copy its settings.

Right-click on a card to paste the stored settings.

## Node Cloning

While in world, (with the Card Cloner in hand) shift+right-click on a Node to copy that Node. This copies all cards (settings and placement) and the network settings.

While **not** looking at a node, right-click can be used to change the paste mode. There are two paste modes:

- Node contents: will paste only the card settings and placement
- Network settings: will paste only the network settings (this will also update all connected nodes with these new settings)

While looking at a node, right-click to paste a copied Node.

While **not** looking at a node, shift+right-click will clear any stored settings from the Card Cloner.
